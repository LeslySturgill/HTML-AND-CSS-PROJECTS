Swimming in rivers in Oregon during the summer can be dangerous, even when the weather is hot. Many rivers are fed by snowmelt from the mountains, so the water stays very cold throughout the summer. Cold water can cause cold shock, making it difficult to breathe or swim. River currents can also be stronger and faster than they appear, and hidden rocks, logs, and sudden drop-offs increase the risk of injury. Because of these hazards, it is important to wear a life jacket, avoid swimming alone, and always be aware of river conditions before entering the water.
