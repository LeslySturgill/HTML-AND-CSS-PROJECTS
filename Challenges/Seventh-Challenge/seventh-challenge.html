Directions Portland to Seattle

From Portland, Oregon, take **Interstate 5 North (I-5 N)** toward Seattle.
Stay on I-5 North for about **170 miles**, passing through Vancouver, Kelso,
Centralia, Olympia, and Tacoma.
Continue following signs for Seattle as you approach the city, where traffic is often heavier,
especially between Tacoma and downtown.

Once you reach Seattle, remain on I-5 and take the exit that best matches your destination.
For downtown Seattle, common exits include **Exit 165A (James Street)**,
**Exit 165B (Union Street/Convention Center)**, and **Exit 166 (Stewart Street/Denny Way)**.
The drive is about **175 miles** and typically takes **2 hours 45 minutes to 3½ hours**,
depending on traffic and road conditions.
